## Cammello 0.12.14

License and copyright status are dropdowns now.

### What is in them
The license template and the structured license (P275) each offer CC0 1.0,
CC BY 4.0 and CC BY-SA 4.0; the copyright status offers "copyrighted".
Entries read as licenses ("CC BY-SA 4.0 ({{Cc-by-sa-4.0}})"); picking one
puts the bare value in the field, so settings, description export and
upload see exactly the same string as before.

### They keep each other honest
The template and the P275 item are the same fact in two forms and must not
disagree. Picking a license in EITHER dropdown now sets the other to the
matching value. Typed text is left alone: entering an unusual template must
not silently rewrite a Q-number.

All three fields stay editable - the presets are the common cases, not a
restriction.

### On the Q-numbers
Verified against wikidata.org: Q18199165 (CC BY-SA 4.0), Q20007257
(CC BY 4.0), Q6938433 (CC0), Q50423863 (copyrighted). Q73566113 ("work
available with a Creative Commons license") and Q19652 (public domain)
were checked on Commons by Harald after my own lookup came up empty - the
copyright status now offers all three with their meanings, and Q73566113
stays the default, which is the correct one for own photographs published
under a CC license.
