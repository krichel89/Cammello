## Cammello 0.12.13

Two additions to the MediaWiki tooltips.

### The property number comes first
The five properties Cammello asks for now open their own tooltip:
P180 (depicts), P10408 (created during), P170 (creator), P275 (license),
P6216 (copyright status). Reading a property number somewhere and wanting
to know which field it belongs to no longer requires guessing.

### The double concept, spelled out
Commons stores every file twice: as wikitext (Information template,
license template, author line) and as structured data (machine-readable
statements). Author and P170 are the same fact; license template and P275
are the same fact; caption and Information description are the same text
in two forms. Nobody guesses that from the field names, so each tooltip
now names its counterpart and says which of the two halves it is - and
where the two must agree, it says so.

P6216 is the exception and says so: copyright status exists only as
structured data, with no wikitext counterpart.

The caption rows of the captions editor got tooltips for the first time
(caption = structured half, Information text = wikitext half).

### The section headings say what their scope is
Three blocks, three ranges - and that is the part the headings do not
convey. Hovering them now says so in Harald's own words: "Author and
license" is what probably stays the same for a photographer forever;
"Base description" covers one upload session, e.g. all pictures of one
event; "Selected file(s)" is the subject of one picture, possibly of
several. The tooltip sits on the header button as well, since that is
what a reader points at.
