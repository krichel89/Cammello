## Cammello 0.12.11

Tooltips for the MediaWiki module - each one explains what BELONGS in the
field: what kind of value, in what format, with a concrete example, and
where it ends up on Commons.

The Wikidata fields got the most attention. depicts says to enter the
shown person's item, that names can be typed and picked from the live
suggestions, and that the field is required unless the override below
gives a reason. "Created during" warns to take the specific edition
("Berlinale 2026"), not the series. The structured license and copyright
fields finally explain their cryptic defaults: Q18199165 is CC BY-SA 4.0
and must match the license template; Q73566113 means "copyrighted" and is
right for own photographs.

The row labels carry the same texts - someone wondering what "P180" means
points at the label, not the input. All texts exist in all five UI
languages.

Why this stays uncluttered: nothing was added to the layout and nothing
moved - no info icons, no help buttons, no extra rows. A tooltip occupies
zero pixels until the pointer rests on a field and disappears on the
first movement.
